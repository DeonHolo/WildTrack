# 06 - Adviser My Teams review

**What to build:** Migrate adviser review around assigned teams and group outputs rather than showing duplicate member submissions as independent adviser work. Retain individual records for grading and audit while giving advisers the shortest path to the team's usable deliverable.

**Blocked by:** 02 - Role-specific application shells; 05 - Deliverable-first Review.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A regular adviser sees only teams assigned to that adviser in the active academic workspace.
- [ ] Sir sees his own assigned teams through the same `My advised teams` workflow while retaining institution-wide access elsewhere.
- [ ] Team selection is visually persistent and exposes deliverables with received-member counts and the current group output.
- [ ] Equivalent member submissions are grouped for adviser work without deleting or merging their individual records.
- [ ] The adviser can open submitted files, view current Document Check results, view an existing AI Review, provide feedback, accept, and unaccept the group output.
- [ ] Advisers cannot run or rerun AI Review.
- [ ] Saved feedback has comfortable control spacing, clear author and timestamp, and a collapsed presentation when long.
- [ ] Student-facing feedback becomes visible to the response owner without exposing adviser-only or admin-only information.
- [ ] Empty assignments, unavailable files, conflicting member submissions, and no-feedback states are explicitly designed.
- [ ] Tests verify adviser scoping, team grouping, feedback visibility, acceptance behavior, and AI permission boundaries.
