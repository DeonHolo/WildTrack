# 10 - Command Center and cross-workflow queues

**What to build:** Rebuild the Command Center as Sir's concise `Today's work` view. It should surface unresolved items from submissions, Document Check, review, identity conflicts, imports, and archive operations without duplicating destination pages or showing low-value activity noise.

**Blocked by:** 05 - Deliverable-first Review; 06 - Adviser My Teams review; 07 - Class Tracker at scale; 08 - Workspace, imports, and templates; 09 - Archive index.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The default view prioritizes compact actionable rows rather than metric cards or shortcut panels.
- [ ] Queue categories include only states Sir can understand and act on, with counts and the relevant destination context.
- [ ] Checked, accepted, resolved, or archived items leave their active queue immediately.
- [ ] Row actions either complete the task in place or open the destination with the correct item selected; they do not land on a generic page requiring a second search.
- [ ] Identity conflicts and import problems appear only when actual conflicts or problems exist.
- [ ] Recent activity is omitted unless an event provides a direct operational decision.
- [ ] The all-clear state is concise and may later host student-independent brand artwork, but no temporary mascot graphic is added in this ticket.
- [ ] Tests verify queue membership, removal after actions, destination context, empty state, role access, and realistic workload volume.
