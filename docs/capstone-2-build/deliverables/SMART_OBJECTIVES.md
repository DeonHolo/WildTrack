# SMART objectives and measurement model

**Status:** proposed research design for the WildTrack MVP evaluation round. The owner approved a post-benchmark clarification of Objective 1's scope on 2026-09-21; see `GOAL1_SCOPE_AMENDMENT_20260921.md`. The technical Goal 1 component run and separate Goal 2 pilot have recorded results, but framework endorsement and real participant/Objective 3 evidence remain outstanding.

The measurement structure follows Victor R. Basili's 1992 Goal/Question/Metric (GQM) report, *Software Modeling and Measurement: The Goal/Question/Metric Paradigm*, University of Maryland Technical Report CS-TR-2956 / UMIACS-TR-92-96: https://www.cs.umd.edu/~basili/publications/technical/T78.pdf. GQM is used here as a goal-led measurement design, not as a claim that these questionnaire items form a validated psychometric scale.

## Objective 1 - Document Check component classification agreement

**SMART objective (owner-approved scope clarification dated 2026-09-21).** By the close of the MVP evaluation round, measure WildTrack's production Document Check PDF inspection, readability, and template-comparison components against the frozen project-defined reference for all 25 pre-specified synthetic STD benchmark families, including their 30 documented conditions and 52 applicable atomic classifications. Achieve at least 90% agreement with that reference, reporting execution coverage, classification errors, per-signal false positives/false negatives and precision/recall where defined, and all failed or unassessable scheduled classifications. This is a **controlled component benchmark**, not a claim about full deployed Google Drive transport, user permissions, all real student PDFs, or general academic correctness.

**GQM framing.** Analyze the Document Check output for the purpose of evaluation, with respect to technical and deterministic classification accuracy, from the viewpoint of the researchers and technical reviewers, in the frozen synthetic STD fixture environment.

**Measurement unit and denominator.**

- Use the prospectively frozen manifest and project-defined expected labels for the designated 25-family/30-condition, 52-assertion component run. Its applicability is limited to actual PDF inspection/template signals and explicitly declared in-memory gateway simulation cases; a simulated Drive classification is not evidence of a live Google Drive failure.
- Let N_DC be the number of applicable frozen assertions scheduled across the benchmark manifest.
- Let C_DC be the number of those assertions whose observed classification matches the frozen expected classification.
- Primary accuracy is DC_accuracy = C_DC / N_DC. An execution failure that prevents a scheduled assertion from producing a classification remains in N_DC and is not counted as correct.
- Where a binary flag has a meaningful positive/negative label, also report TP, TN, FP, FN, precision TP/(TP+FP), recall TP/(TP+FN), false-positive rate FP/(FP+TN), and false-negative rate FN/(FN+TP). Report a metric as not estimable when its denominator is zero.
- Report execution coverage separately as N_DC_completed / N_DC, plus the exact counts and reasons for corrupt or non-PDF files, parser failures, simulated gateway outcomes, or other unassessable observations. Do not report simulated Drive/API outcomes as live provider observations.

**Scope and evidence.** This goal uses team-run synthetic technical fixtures and the production PDF inspector/template comparator, with four conditions including declared simulated gateway behavior through the production FileCheckService. Participant opinions, optional live-workflow screenshots, and anecdotal successful Drive checks are not part of the 52-assertion accuracy denominator. Participants do not grade PDFs or author benchmark fixtures. The official STD template and supplied STD instructions are the authority for requirement-backed expectations. Generic standards knowledge must not be promoted into an expected requirement that the supplied authority does not contain.

**Reference and interpretation.** The 25-family/30-condition technical reference uses researcher/project-defined expected labels prepared with AI assistance from actual synthetic PDFs and supplied authority; there is **no required independent human label review** and no independently verified ground truth. The distinct, source-frozen component run recorded 52/52 matching project-reference classifications and 52/52 execution coverage; see `../benchmarks/std/results/goal1-project-reference-r2-20260921/README.md`. Its original result status, raw observations, hash key and historical development probe are retained without relabeling or rewriting. The 90% numerical threshold is met **for the clarified component scope only**. The original broader end-to-end wording was narrowed *after* this benchmark, so this is not a blind prospective evaluation of a newly declared goal; the adviser must endorse the revised framework/scope for academic acceptance. No additional live Drive-path benchmark is a Goal 1 completion requirement under the clarified objective. Do not classify unmeasured semantic, live provider or real-student scenarios as successes.

**Threshold rationale.** The 90% threshold is a transparent proposed MVP gate for the defined component benchmark, not a published standard or adviser-approved target. The recorded component run exceeds it against its project-defined reference; this does not establish independently validated or population-wide accuracy. Keeping execution failures in the primary denominator prevents an apparently strong score produced only by dropping hard cases.

## Objective 2 - AI Review grounded-content screening

**SMART objective.** By the close of the MVP evaluation round, attempt one fresh provider-backed **AI Review component** call per fixture, only when the free-tier quota permits, on the frozen ten-fixture pilot: STD-01, STD-02, STD-03, STD-05, STD-08, STD-09, STD-10, STD-12, STD-18, and STD-21. Compare fresh reports with a **researcher-defined, PDF-grounded reference checklist** frozen before observing provider results. The proposed pilot targets are at least 85% checklist-decision agreement and at least 90% substantive-claim traceability, conditional on adjudicable fresh reports. Report fresh-run coverage, cache hits or absence of cache evidence, quota/provider/transport failures, unassessable decisions, unsupported claims, and case-level evidence separately. Do not use paid fallback, automatic retries, or better-of-several runs.

**GQM framing.** Analyze the stored AI Review report for the purpose of a scoped pilot evaluation, with respect to agreement with a frozen researcher-defined checklist and evidence-supported content claims, from the viewpoint of the researchers, in a synthetic low-budget STD pilot. This is not independent expert validation.

**Measurement unit and denominator.**

- The eleven project-defined issue/clean reference decisions have been prepared using an AI-assisted review of the ten actual extracted PDF source texts and supplied authority; `ai-checklist.csv` records evidence excerpts/rationales and `reference_method=PROJECT_AI_ASSISTED_PDF_AND_AUTHORITY_REVIEW` with `reference_status=FROZEN_PROJECT_DEFINED`. This does **not** represent an independent human or user-performed manual source audit. Before inspecting any official pilot provider output, preserve the prepared key/manifest and record ten fixture and template hashes, `instructions_sha256`, `protocol_sha256`, app commit/prompt/model version and real run-level `frozen_at`; that dated fingerprint is not claimed as already completed here. Earlier development/smoke observations must be disclosed and excluded from the official ten-fixture pilot rather than described as unseen.
- Let N_AI_ATTEMPT = 10 planned fixture attempts. Let N_AI_FRESH_OK be the number that complete as fresh provider runs with a review report. Fresh-run coverage is N_AI_FRESH_OK / 10.
- Let N_AI_DEC be the number of adjudicable frozen checklist decisions in fresh successful reports, and C_AI_DEC the number whose observed decision matches the frozen researcher-defined expectation. The reported `checklist_agreement` is C_AI_DEC / N_AI_DEC; report the number and reasons for unassessable decisions separately. Agreement with this reference is **not** an independently validated AI accuracy rate.
- Let N_AI_CLAIM be the number of distinct substantive finding claims emitted in fresh successful reports, including document facts, mismatch or requirement assertions, and requested actions. Audit **every** claim against its exact report passage and the source PDF or explicitly mapped deliverable instructions/template, recording a source location/excerpt, support judgment, reviewer and uncertainty. Let C_AI_TRACE be claims with adequately documented source support. Claim traceability is C_AI_TRACE / N_AI_CLAIM; report unsupported and uncertain claims and examples separately. An assertion of a mandatory requirement needs supplied authority, not general knowledge or a confident model statement.
- Cache hits, quota failures, provider failures, invalid responses, and runs with no fresh report do not enter the decision-agreement or claim-traceability denominators, because they produced no fresh scored report. They remain in the fixed ten-attempt coverage denominator and must be reported separately rather than silently removed.
- Report any metric as not estimable when its denominator is zero.

**Scope and evidence.** This is a researcher-scored, AI-assisted project-reference evaluation of **synthetic STD fixtures**, not an independent human/expert review, independently verified ground truth, a measurement of accuracy on real student submissions, or a claim that software tests in the fixtures actually ran. The technical pilot runner invokes the actual `GeminiAiReviewProvider.review` using `AiReviewService.SYSTEM_INSTRUCTION` and the configured model/format; it does **not** exercise the complete deployed Admin/Drive/cache/UI request path or the private `AiReviewService.groundAndValidate` post-filter. Findings therefore describe this provider-backed component under the controlled pilot configuration, **not** full production end-to-end AI Review performance. AI Review is advisory. Requirement-backed findings must come from the configured deliverable instructions or official template; PDF-grounded observations may identify document facts or an obvious wrong-deliverable mismatch. General domain knowledge cannot become a mandatory WildTrack requirement without supplied authority. In STD-18, no official template is mapped for the model: do not judge a claimed template requirement as supported by an authority it did not receive. Preserve the frozen reference and raw report evidence; label any later key changes as post-observation amendments, not a pre-run reference.

**Threshold rationale.** The 85% decision-agreement and 90% claim-traceability targets are proposed descriptive pilot gates, not published standards, adviser-approved thresholds, achieved results, or proof of generalizable AI accuracy. Report small denominators and uncertainty explicitly even if either threshold is met.

## Objective 3 - Student submission transaction correctness

**SMART objective — revised after the questionnaire was distributed (2026-09-22).** By the close of the MVP validation period, verify that at least **95% of eligible, genuinely consenting students' assessable *initial saved Refactored SRS response records*** in the active Semester 1 workspace preserve the correct student, workspace, deliverable, original saved record and required PDF link/stored values. A record passes only if the **five primary persistence/association assertions** are evidenced. Observed student-visible readback is supporting evidence, **not** another required past screenshot. This measures **correctness of selected, persisted initial records**, NOT success across all started/failed/abandoned attempts; no student is required to edit a response. See `GOAL3_INITIAL_SUBMISSION_AMENDMENT_20260922.md` for the explicit post-distribution change, consent restrictions, historical/evidence limitations and separate researcher technical checks.

**GQM framing.** Analyze actual initial saved student submission records for the purpose of evaluation, with respect to persisted-record correctness, from the viewpoint of eligible consenting students and researchers, in the active WildTrack MVP validation workspace.

**Measurement unit and denominator.**

- Do **not** schedule blank-link attempts or revisions for the student cohort. Select a defensible cohort of **one genuine initial saved Refactored SRS record per eligible student**, record actual consent permitting analysis of the selected record, and preserve the pre/post-amendment observation date. A generic Form consent referring only to a separate controlled task must not silently authorize unrelated natural submission records.
- **N_INITIAL** = all eligible initial saved records selected under the declared inclusion rule with valid research consent, including selected records with a FAIL or UNVERIFIED assertion. **C_INITIAL** = selected records for which all required, independently evidenced persistence/association checks pass. `INITIAL_SAVED_CORRECTNESS = C_INITIAL / N_INITIAL`, not estimable when N=0.
- Report each assertion's PASS/FAIL/UNVERIFIED status, the number of already-viewed records, missing first-version evidence, and student-visible readback limitations. A current `Revised submission` choice with revision 1 and no actual initial history does not prove T2 or original initial values.
- An invalid attempt that never persisted is **outside** this saved-record denominator. Report independently observed failed/abandoned attempts separately; do not present this measure as success over all submission attempts.
- Test blank-link rejection, non-PDF/DOCX handling and edit/revision correctness separately using researcher-controlled synthetic scenarios. Their results do not add students or records to N_INITIAL.

**Scope and evidence.** Objective 3 uses actual initial saved WildTrack version/association evidence and a separately reviewed restricted researcher log; the original temporary Admin `Validation Study` overallPass is the old T1/T2-only diagnostic and must not be used as the new Goal 3 score. The published Refactored SRS `Validation step` field may remain for ordinary workflow; it is **not** a research requirement to choose `Revised submission`. Questionnaire self-report and 1–5 clarity responses are descriptive only, not evidence of persisted correctness. The already distributed Google Form and its original `Form_Responses` tab are not replaced.

**Threshold rationale and chronology.** The earlier two-task objective was documented and the Form distributed **before** this initial-saved-record amendment. The original 95% threshold is retained as a proposed working gate for a *different, narrower metric*; it is not adviser-approved and cannot retroactively turn already-observed responses into prospective evidence. Preserve the old protocol in archives, fix the revised inclusion/scoring rules before selecting more records, and obtain academic agreement on the scope and consent.

## Planned sample, endpoint, and exclusions

The course validation plan requires at least 30 unique consenting stakeholder participants across varied roles. The working composition is approximately 27-28 students, 1-2 advisers, and one Admin/beneficiary. This is a planned minimum, not a completed sample or a claim of population representativeness.

The evaluation endpoint is the close of the MVP evaluation round after questionnaire role routing, the **revised** Objective 3 initial-record inclusion/codebook rules, applicable consent and duplicate rules, independently checked initial saved records and existing technical benchmark logs have been documented. The originally distributed questionnaire/old T1/T2 protocol remain identifiable historical versions. Sir Ralph Laviste's September 14 consultation transcript remains one real qualitative consultation source and is never inserted as a fabricated questionnaire row.

Decliners do not count as participants. Advisers and Admin/beneficiaries do not enter the student Objective 3 transaction denominator. Questionnaire free-text feedback is optional. No observed completion-time measure is required for any objective.
